**Full description** (4,000 characters at most):

```
RouteForge makes your Android device report a simulated location, so you can test location-based apps without moving. Plan a route on the map, choose how it is followed, and play it back at the speed you want.

It is a tool for developers and testers. You turn it on by choosing RouteForge as the mock location app in Android's Developer options; a guided setup inside the app walks you through it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Real roads or straight lines, your choice: Guided follows real streets (worked out on your phone, offline once the map data is downloaded); Free-roam goes straight through your points.
• Realistic movement: speed, bearing and accuracy stay consistent with each other.
• Joystick: move freely in any direction at any time.
• Edit a route while it plays: reorder, add, move or delete the waypoints still ahead of you.
• Control it from the notification: pause, resume or stop, even from the lock screen.
• Search for a place by name, or open and share places and routes from other apps: map links, Google Maps shares and GPX files.
• Save favorite places and whole routes. Import and export routes as GPX or JSON.
• Playback options: speed up to 150 km/h; play once, N times, in a loop or back and forth.
• Choose the map style (Standard or Topographic) and decide how much storage the offline map data may use.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your real location stays on your device. Map pictures, place search and map downloads use public services (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Nominatim and BRouter); the privacy policy lists exactly what they receive.

GOOD TO KNOW
• While a simulation runs, other apps on your device see the simulated location. Use RouteForge responsibly and follow the rules of the services you test.
• Search, map pictures and map downloads need an internet connection. Routes work offline once their region is downloaded.
• Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Privacy policy: https://routeforge.andgabx.dev/privacy/
```

## Screenshot plan (phone, portrait)

Use fictional or famous public places, never the maintainer's real position, favorites or saved routes. Turn on do-not-disturb
and use the same device and theme for all of them.

1. Simulate tab: a route playing along real roads, the position marker and the playback card.
2. Plan Route tab: waypoints placed on the map, with the Guided and Free-roam choice.
3. Search: results for a place name, the first one ready to be added or teleported to.
4. Saved tab: a couple of saved routes and locations (made-up names).
5. Settings: map style and the offline storage limit, or the region catalog.
6. The notification with Pause, Resume and Stop over the lock screen.
